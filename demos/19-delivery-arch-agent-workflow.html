Integrated Delivery Architecture Agent Workflow

Agents are connected components of one workflow, not independent Copilot tools. Each stage progressively increases response maturity.

One connected workflow progressively matures the response. Agents prepare evidence and artefacts; accountable humans approve commitments.

Delivery Resource Intelligence is retained downstream to support mobilisation after commercial commitment.
